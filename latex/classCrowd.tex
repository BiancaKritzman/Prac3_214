\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Represents the crowd and capacity of a music zone. 

\doxylink{classCrowd}{Crowd} extends \doxylink{classMusicZone}{Music\+Zone} with maximum and current capacity information and reports when the zone is approaching or exceeding its capacity.
