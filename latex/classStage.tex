\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Represents a festival performance stage. 

\doxylink{classStage}{Stage} is a leaf participant in the Composite pattern because it cannot contain child components.

It also acts as an Observer and responds to theft and merchandise alerts affecting the stage.
