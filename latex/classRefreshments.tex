\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Represents a refreshments vendor in the festival. 

\doxylink{classRefreshments}{Refreshments} is a leaf component within the \doxylink{classVendorZone}{Vendor\+Zone} hierarchy and stores information about the vendor and available refreshments.
