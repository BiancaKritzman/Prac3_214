\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Represents a festival zone that can contain other components. 

\doxylink{classZones}{Zones} is the Composite participant in the Composite design pattern. It allows individual festival components and groups of components to be treated through the same \doxylink{classMusicFestivalObserver}{Music\+Festival\+Observer} interface.

\doxylink{classZones}{Zones} also acts as a Subject in the Observer pattern by maintaining a list of observers and notifying them when a festival event occurs.
